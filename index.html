Common questions

Quick answers before you buy.

How long until my data arrives?

Your data is credited within a few minutes of us receiving your order and payment.

Which networks do you support?

Vodacom, MTN, Telkom and Cell C. Choose the network the number belongs to, because data is credited to that network.

How can I pay?

Pay by EFT deposit to the bank account shown at checkout, or redeem an OTT, 1Voucher, BluVoucher or AnyTime voucher.

What are Anytime and Night Owl data?

Each bundle is split in two. Anytime data can be used at any time of day, and Night Owl data is for off-peak night-time hours.

Can I top up someone else’s number?

Yes. Enter their number and network at checkout. Check both carefully, because we credit exactly what you enter.
